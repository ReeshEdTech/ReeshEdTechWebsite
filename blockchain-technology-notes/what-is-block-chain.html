1. Need of Blockchain Technology

Why do we need blockchain? Let's understand through the story of Techgaon.

Imagine a village led by a clerk who manages all records. If the clerk becomes corrupt, the entire system fails. Blockchain solves this by distributing the ledger to everyone, making it "trustless" - you trust the system, not a person.

Key Reasons for Blockchain:

  • ✅ Decentralization: No single point of failure or control.
  • ✅ Transparency: Everyone sees the same data.
  • ✅ Security: Cryptographically locked against tampering.
  • ✅ Immutability: Once data is written, it cannot be changed.

2. What is Blockchain?

A blockchain is a decentralized, distributed ledger that records transactions across many computers so that the record cannot be altered retroactively.

Core Components of a Block

  • 1. Data: The actual information (transactions, contracts).
  • 2. Timestamp: When the block verified.
  • 3. Nonce: Random number for mining proof.
  • 4. Hash (Self): Unique digital signature of the block.
  • 5. Previous Hash: Link to the previous block.

3. Blockchain Layers

Application, Execution, Semantic, Propagation, and Consensus layers make up the blockchain architecture. Each layer serves a distinct purpose in the ecosystem.

4. Consensus Layer

The mechanism by which nodes agree on the validity of transactions. Examples include Proof of Work (PoW) used by Bitcoin and Proof of Stake (PoS).
