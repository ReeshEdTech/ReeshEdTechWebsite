Your Grades Won't Open Job Doors — But Your GitHub Repo Will!

Why proof of work matters more than your CGPA in 2025.

In 2025, your college CGPA might still impress your relatives, but recruiters? Not so much.

Today’s tech hiring is all about skills, projects, collaboration, and most importantly — proof of work. And there’s no better proof than your GitHub portfolio.

The Harsh Truth: Grades Aren’t Enough in 2025

Here’s a bitter pill:

“A 9.0 GPA with no practical experience loses out to a 6.5 GPA with real-world projects on GitHub.”

College exams test theory, not problem-solving. Real-world jobs need code, not crammed answers.

What Recruiters Actually Look For

✅ Skills ❌ Grades
Clean code on GitHub CGPA out of 10
Real-world projects Lab assignments
Open-source contributions Semester toppers
Collaboration (PRs, issues) Attendance records
Problem-solving on LeetCode Rank in class

Why GitHub is Your New Resume

Your GitHub profile tells a story:

  • What languages do you know?
  • How active are you in coding?
  • Are you a team player (open source PRs)?
  • Can you build full-stack apps, scripts, tools?

💡 Pro Tip: Many recruiters search candidates directly on GitHub and filter by language, stars, and contribution graph.

How to Build a Powerful GitHub Profile (Action Plan)

  1. Start Building Projects – Weather app, Chat app, Portfolio site
  2. Contribute to Open Source – Look for “good first issues”
  3. Be Consistent – Green graph matters
  4. Link GitHub Everywhere – Resume, LinkedIn, Website

Final Thoughts: Build Projects, Not Just CGPA

Your grades may help clear eligibility filters, but they don’t prove your skills.

🎯 “So next time you study for an exam, spend an hour on GitHub too. Your future self will thank you.”
