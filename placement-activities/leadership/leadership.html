Leadership Assessment Test

Choose the most appropriate response for each scenario.

1. A group project is failing due to miscommunication. What do you do?

2. A teammate is underperforming. What is your response?

3. Your team disagrees with your suggestion. What do you do?

4. The deadline is close, and your team is behind. What do you do?

5. You’re asked to lead a new initiative. How do you react?

6. Someone disagrees strongly during a discussion. What’s your response?

7. The team succeeds in a major task. What do you do?

8. You see a conflict rising between two teammates. What’s your action?

9. You have to present a project on behalf of the team. What’s your approach?

10. You’re overwhelmed with tasks. What do you do?
